Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 October 2026

This policy explains how The Vellore District Consumers Coop Wholesale Stores Limited ("Karpagam Super Market", "we", "us") collects and uses your personal data when you use our Android app and place orders for home delivery in Vellore, Tamil Nadu.

1. Data we collect

  • Account: mobile number (used to log in with an OTP), name, and email if you give one.
  • Delivery addresses: address text and the map location (latitude and longitude) you pin. We use your location only when you choose to pin an address or use "my location"; we do not track you in the background.
  • Orders: items, amounts, delivery slot, order status, delivery person's name and phone, and payment status (not your card, UPI PIN or bank details).
  • Support: messages you send us.
  • Device: a push-notification token so we can tell you about your order.

2. Why we use it

  • To log you in, take and deliver your order, and check that your address is inside our delivery area.
  • To send order updates by app notification, SMS or WhatsApp.
  • To take payments, give refunds and keep accounting and GST records.
  • To answer support requests and keep the service safe.
  • Offers and announcements, only if you have agreed; you can turn these off at any time.

3. Who we share it with

We do not sell your data. We share only what is needed with service providers acting for us:

  • Supabase (database and file storage hosting)
  • Google Firebase (phone verification and push notifications) and Google Maps (map display)
  • Razorpay (online payments, once enabled)
  • MSG91 (SMS and WhatsApp order messages, once enabled)
  • Our own delivery staff, who see your name, address and phone for the order.

We may disclose data if required by law or a court order.

4. Payments

Online payments are processed by Razorpay. We never see or store your card number, UPI PIN or netbanking credentials. Cash on Delivery is paid to our delivery staff.

5. How long we keep it

Order and billing records are kept for the period required by Indian tax and accounting law. Other data is kept while your account is active and deleted or anonymised after you ask us to delete the account, unless the law requires us to keep it.

6. Your rights

You can ask us to show, correct or delete your personal data, withdraw consent for messages, or close your account. Email or call us using the details below. We will reply within a reasonable time and in any case within the time the law allows (Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023).

7. Security

Data is transferred over encrypted connections and stored in access-controlled systems; staff accounts are limited by role. No system is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your data.

8. Children

The app is meant for adults. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 18 without a parent's consent.

9. Changes

We may update this policy; the date above shows the latest version. Significant changes will be shown in the app.
