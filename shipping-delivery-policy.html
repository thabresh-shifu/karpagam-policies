Shipping and Delivery Policy

Last updated: [DATE]

1. Where we deliver

We deliver only within about 10 km of our store at No. 14, Officers Line, Vellore 632001. Pin your address on the map in the app; if it is outside the delivery area the app will tell you and the order cannot be placed. We do not ship outside Vellore or by courier.

2. How delivery works

Our own staff deliver your order. There is no third-party courier. Choose a delivery date and time slot at checkout (for example 10 AM to 1 PM or 4 PM to 7 PM; the slots shown in the app are current). This is regular scheduled delivery, not instant or 10-minute delivery.

3. Order status

You can follow your order in the app: placed, confirmed, packing, out for delivery, delivered. You will receive a notification when the status changes. When the order is out for delivery, the delivery person's name and phone number are shown on the order.

4. Charges

Delivery charge and minimum order value are set by the store and shown in your cart before you pay. They may change from time to time; the amount shown when you place the order applies. [FILL: current delivery charge, free-delivery limit, minimum order value]

5. Delivery time

We aim to deliver within the slot you chose. Delays can happen due to weather, traffic, festival days or high demand; we will try to inform you. If we cannot deliver on the chosen day we will contact you to agree a new slot or cancel with a full refund.

6. Receiving your order

  • Please be available at the address and keep your phone on. The delivery person may call you.
  • If we cannot reach you after reasonable attempts, the order may be cancelled. Prepaid amounts are refunded as set out in the Refund and Cancellation Policy.
  • For Cash on Delivery, please pay the bill amount in cash or by UPI if the delivery person accepts it.
  • Please check the items at the door and report any problem straight away.
