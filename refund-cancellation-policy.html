Refund and Cancellation Policy

Last updated: [DATE]

1. Cancelling an order

  • You can ask to cancel an order before it is marked "out for delivery". Call us on [STORE PHONE] or use Support in the app. Staff cancel the order for you.
  • Once an order is out for delivery it cannot be cancelled, but you may refuse items at the door that do not match the order (see section 3).
  • We may cancel an order if an item is out of stock, the address is outside our delivery area or cannot be reached, or the order cannot be delivered in the chosen slot. We will tell you, and you pay nothing for a cancelled order.

2. Refunds for online payments

  • If you paid online and the order is cancelled, or an item is missing or out of stock, we refund the amount to the original payment method (UPI, card or bank).
  • Refunds are started within 2 working days of the cancellation or approved return, and usually reach your account within 5 to 7 working days, depending on your bank.
  • If money was debited but the order was not placed, it is refunded automatically by the payment provider, usually within 5 to 7 working days. Contact us if it does not arrive.
  • For Cash on Delivery orders no money is taken before delivery, so a cancelled order costs you nothing. If we owe you a refund after delivery (for example a damaged item), we refund it in cash on request or to your bank/UPI account.

3. Damaged, wrong, missing or expired items

  • Please check your order when it arrives. If an item is damaged, wrong, short, or past its expiry date, tell the delivery person, or contact us within 24 hours of delivery with the order number and, if possible, a photo.
  • We will, at your choice, replace the item or refund its price (and the delivery charge if the whole order was our mistake).
  • Perishable goods (fruits, vegetables, dairy, bakery, meat, frozen items) are not returnable once delivered unless they were spoiled or wrong when delivered.

4. Items we cannot take back

  • Opened or used items, unless defective.
  • Items without original packaging or seals, where the product is food or personal care.
  • Items you simply changed your mind about, after delivery.
